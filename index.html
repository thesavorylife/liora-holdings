About the Company

Structure that supports growth

Liora Holdings Inc is a privately held holding company incorporated in the State of Wyoming, with its principal office at 211 W 19th Street, Suite 103-1081A, Cheyenne, WY 82001.

The Company serves as an intermediate holding entity providing a structured layer that supports clear governance, liability separation, and efficient capital stewardship across the portfolio.

Consulting services are advisory in nature. Clients are encouraged to seek appropriately licensed professionals for legal and financial matters. All fees are billed in United States Dollars (USD).

Our Vision

To serve as a disciplined and transparent holding structure that strengthens the integrity of the Liora enterprise, preserving the long-term interests of its affiliated entities and the stakeholders they serve.

Our Mission

To maintain a sound and well-governed intermediate holding structure that supports asset protection, organizational clarity, and the sustained growth of each operating entity within the Liora portfolio.

Our Purpose

The role we serve

01

Governance & Structure

Liora Holdings Inc provides a clear and deliberate corporate layer that supports accountability, organized oversight, and defined lines of authority across affiliated operating companies.

02

Asset Stewardship

The Company facilitates the organized holding and management of interests in its affiliated entities, ensuring proper separation and protection of assets within the broader enterprise structure.

03

Portfolio Continuity

By maintaining a stable intermediate holding structure, Liora Holdings Inc supports the long-term continuity and scalability of the Liora enterprise portfolio as it grows and evolves.
