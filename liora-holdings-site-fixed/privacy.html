Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Liora Holdings Inc ("Liora Holdings," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit lioraholdingsinc.com (the "Site") or engage our services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: your name, organization, email address, and the contents of any message you send us.
  • Payment information: when you begin a retainer, your card details are entered directly into a secure form provided by Stripe, Inc. Card numbers are sent to Stripe and are never stored on our servers.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider may automatically log basic information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and site operation.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To process payments, manage subscriptions, and send billing records.
  • To maintain the security and operation of the Site.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Site and deliver our services, such as Stripe (payment processing), Netlify (website hosting), and our email provider. These providers may use the information only to perform services for us. We may also disclose information when required by law.

4. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain business and tax records, and resolve disputes, after which it is deleted or securely archived.

5. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. Payments are processed by Stripe, which is certified to PCI DSS standards. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to our legal and record-keeping obligations. To make a request, email info@lioraholdingsinc.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site and our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
